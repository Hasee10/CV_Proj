## Notes
- The GNHK subset was **not** rebuilt. Its manifest hash is unchanged, and it passes the stricter validator (0 near-duplicate candidates): `VALIDATION_GNHK.json`.
- A finding from testing: synthetic blank sheets produce many cross-split near-duplicate candidates, because empty boxes look alike. The validator correctly blocks until they are reviewed. Real data with blank fields may need the same review.
- The legacy `scripts/m3b_metrics.py` is unchanged and kept only to reproduce M3-B artifacts. Formal evaluation uses `eval_metrics.py`.
- `artifacts/M4/VALIDATION_GNHK.json` (old validator) is preserved; the re-validation is in this folder.
- Real-photo extraction is **not verified**; it awaits the W01_A pilot.
